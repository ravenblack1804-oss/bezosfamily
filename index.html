Connecting digital infrastructure with global markets.

One of the major long-term objectives for the Bezos Family ecosystem is the development of a powerful bridge between digital assets and participation in globally recognized public markets.

BZF STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE
1 BZF → 10 Amazon Shares

The Bezos Family roadmap is being shaped around an ambitious target framework designed around a relationship of 1 BZF to 10 Amazon shares.

The vision is to build the infrastructure capable of connecting Stellar-based digital participation with traditional markets through the brokerage, custody and compliance systems required for large-scale participation.

This represents one of the major long-term milestones in the Bezos Family roadmap and part of a broader strategy centered around digital infrastructure and global market access.
